tags: ["coding"]

---

Create a Markdown file in `src/blog/`. Copy this post's frontmatter and change the
title, date, description, and tags. Write your content below the frontmatter.

The filename becomes the URL. Set `draft: false` when you are ready to publish;
drafts appear only in development. Use `kind: personal` for posts that should
appear on the blog but stay out of the home page's latest engineering posts.

You can delete this example once you have written your first post.
